The short version

Make systems make sense.

A design system is never only its components — it's a living body of data: tokens, constraints, conventions, and the decisions behind them. Our tools make that data legible and dependable, so it can be worked with in editors, pipelines, and governance processes alike.

We are deliberately not another format or platform. Design Lasagna works with the Design Tokens Community Group standard rather than replacing it.
