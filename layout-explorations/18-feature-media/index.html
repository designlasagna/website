A feature article with video, wide images, and headings.

Use when the article needs a strong lead, then room for media blocks that break up the reading without overwhelming it.

By Design Lasagna8 min readFormat Feature + media

Feature pages often need more than paragraphs. They need a lead image, maybe a video, maybe a chart or a screenshot — all arranged so the article still feels coherent.

Open with one strong media block.

The page should tell you, immediately, what kind of content it supports. That makes it easier to plan a story with a mix of narrative and media.

Why it matters

A feature article needs rhythms: text, media, text, media. This layout shows where those beats can live.

Break the story into sections.

Section headings do the heavy lifting. They keep the article scannable and give each media block a purpose. Without them, the page becomes a gallery instead of an article.

A strong article layout makes media feel like evidence, not decoration.
