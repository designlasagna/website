Writing · photo strip story

A story built from photo strips.

Designed for visual essays and launch stories — the images are full-width chapters, and the prose acts like a calm narrator between them.

Full-bleed imagery gives the page a stronger opening beat. Instead of using a small image as decoration, the picture becomes the stage for the title and the first idea.

Image bandUse it to transition from the opener into the body text.

After the first image, the page can alternate between narrower prose and another full-width visual hit. That creates a feature-article cadence without losing readability.

Keep the images chapter-sized.

The trick is to let each image feel like a section divider, not a random block. The article still reads smoothly, but the visuals do more of the storytelling.

This is where full bleed pays off: the page feels like a story, not a stack of cards.

That makes the layout especially useful for essays, launches, interviews, and any page where the images deserve as much presence as the copy.

Notes

Best for: essays, features, and visual launch stories.

Pattern: bleed hero → image band → text → image band.
