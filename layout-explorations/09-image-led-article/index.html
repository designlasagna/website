Writing · image-led

Let the article use pictures as punctuation.

A more visual article layout for stories, docs intros, and essays that benefit from screenshots, sketches, diagrams, or product shots woven into the reading flow.

Some pages feel better when they alternate between text and image. The eye gets a reset; the reader gets a clearer rhythm. For a project like Design Lasagna, that could mean screenshots of schemas, diagrams of workflows, or visual examples of docs pages.

Use image blocks to change pace.

Rather than stacking long stretches of text, the article can break into sections that each introduce a visual proof. This is especially useful when the page teaches something or explains a workflow.

The layout still reads as an article — it just behaves more like an illustrated essay.

The goal is not decoration — it is clarity through rhythm.

Best for content with examples.

If the article includes screenshots, step photos, charts, or sketches, this layout gives those assets room to breathe without losing the editorial tone.

Notes

Best for: tutorials with screenshots, essays with diagrams, and launch pages with visual proof.

Pattern: title → hero image → text → figure → text → figures.
