Design Lasagna · exploration

Twenty-seven layouts.

Concept explorations for the home page / site structure. Each file is self-contained, uses the current type & colour, and touches nothing in src/. Open one to browse it, then come back and pick (or mix) favourites.

  • 01PosterOne gigantic statement, thin chrome, a single rule and a link strip. Maximum type, minimum furniture.
  • 02Menu / Bento"Today's menu" — every section is a dish card sized by importance (schemas = signature dish).
  • 03Dev SidebarDocs-app feel: permanent left nav, a terminal install panel, right-hand table of contents.
  • 04Recipe / CoursesNumbered courses 01–04: prep (schemas) → cook (language server) → serve (docs) → dine (writing & about).
  • 05Split / StickyPermanent purple side panel with nav & contact; content scrolls past in bold ruled sections.
  • 06Article EssayLong-form writing column with a sticky TOC and editorial side notes.
  • 07Guide PlaybookStep-by-step article layout for tutorials, onboarding, and how-to pages.
  • 08Magazine SpreadA feature-style article with a big hero, summary panels, and editorial notes.
  • 09Image-led ArticleMore images woven through the content, captions included.
  • 10Image MosaicMixed-size collage opening with captions and image rhythm.
  • 11Diptych ArticlePaired image/text sections that alternate as chapters.
  • 12Collage GuideGuide article with layered images, notes, and callouts.
  • 13Full-Bleed OpenerCinematic hero image stretched edge-to-edge with overlay text.
  • 14Bleed EditorialMagazine-style article with alternating full-width image bands.
  • 15Photo Strip StoryRepeated full-bleed image chapters with calm prose between.
  • 16Longform EditorialText-heavy article with headings, tables, and pull quotes.
  • 17Report / ReferenceStructured page for charts, summary stats, and tables.
  • 18Feature / MediaFeature article with video, wide images, and section headings.
  • 19Editorial SpineRecommended default Writing template: a calm article column, discreet TOC rail, and breakout evidence.
  • 20Guided RecipeOutcome-led Learn template with prerequisites, steps, code, troubleshooting, and verification.
  • 21Documentation WorkbenchTechnical reference built around a minimal example, field records, short definition links, and disclosure details.
  • 22Tool Profile + LibraryA product-oriented tool overview followed by grouped paths into Docs, Learn, and Writing.
  • 23Layered ExplainerConcept article with alternating prose, diagrams, and visual chapter breaks.
  • 24Evidence DeskReport layout for key findings, accessible charts, responsive comparison data, and methodology.
  • 25Library IndexGrouped, repeatable content discovery for Docs, Learn, Writing, or topic archives.
  • 26Release NoteVersion-specific changes, breaking-change alerts, migrations, and package links.
  • 27Front DoorHomepage route finder with a clear claim, concrete demo, three paths, and current activity.

These are scratch files for discussion only — not part of the Eleventy build, not linked from the live site. Safe to keep, delete, or promote any of them into src/ later.
