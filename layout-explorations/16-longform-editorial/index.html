A longform article page that can hold real content.

Text-first, with slots for wide figures, pull quotes, tables, and occasional media. The point is to understand the editorial structure before styling it further.

By Design Lasagna6 min readUpdated 2025-01-01
ThesisEvidenceReset the paceNotes

Longform pages need to answer a simple question: what kinds of content might appear inside the article? This exploration lays out the pieces explicitly so the structure is easy to discuss.

We start with prose, then place a wide image where the reader needs a shift in pace. After that come a pull quote, a table, and a second figure — the sort of ingredients a real article might require.

Start with a clear thesis.

The opening paragraphs should explain what the page is doing and why it matters. In a longform article, the reader needs orientation before they encounter details.

“Good article pages organize content before they decorate it.”

Show the supporting evidence.

Some articles need a table instead of another paragraph. Others need a chart or a screenshot. This page reserves space for those blocks instead of hiding them in a generic content stack.

Let one image reset the pace.

After denser text, a second figure can provide relief. The layout keeps the reading column narrow, but lets the image escape that constraint when it needs to.
