About

Why we're
here.

Design Lasagna is open tooling for design systems: tools that make system data understandable, usable, and governable across the software you already use.

A design system is never only its components — it's a living body of data: tokens, constraints, conventions, and the decisions behind them. That data is trapped inside tools, duplicated across teams, and easy to let drift. Our purpose is to make that data legible and dependable, so it can be worked with in editors, pipelines, and governance processes alike.

We are deliberately not another format or platform. Design Lasagna works with open standards such as the Design Tokens Community Group (DTCG) rather than replacing them, and the schemas we publish are plain artifacts you can adopt on your own. The language server is one of several tasty parts, not a monolith you must join.

Principles

What we optimize for

  • Interoperability. Tools that read, write, and exchange standard formats instead of private ones. If it has a DTCG file, it should work here.
  • Clear contracts. Schemas and versioned behavior you can rely on. When the shape of data changes, we say so loudly.
  • Useful editor feedback. Diagnostics, completions, and hints that explain why something is wrong and how to fix it — not just where.
  • Mechanism, not policy. We provide the tools to model, check, and govern your system; the rules themselves stay yours.
