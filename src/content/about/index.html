About Design Lasagna

Design Lasagna builds tools and shares ideas for people building and maintaining design systems.

Why lasagna?

A design system brings together people, practices, governance, documentation, design, and code. Like a lasagna, it is made of layers. How those layers fit together depends on the organization.

It is shared across disciplines—not something the design team owns alone. No single tool, whether Figma or a code repository, defines the whole system.

Different people, workflows, and architectures call for different recipes—not one prescribed stack.
