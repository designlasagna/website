Many layers · no single recipe

Different systems.
Different recipes.

More than a design file. More than a component library.

A design system brings together people, practices, governance, documentation, design, and code. Like a lasagna, it is made of layers. How those layers fit together depends on the organization.

It is shared across disciplines—not something the design team owns alone. No single tool, whether Figma or a code repository, defines the whole system.

Design Lasagna explores ways to make those layers work together: connecting design and code, keeping tokens in sync, and making systems understandable and usable by humans and AI. Different approaches suit different teams; there is no single recipe to sell.
